    House Designer

    • Orbit: left-drag rotate, right-drag pan, scroll zoom.
    • Furniture: pick from the list, drag to move. Q/E rotate, arrows nudge 1 cm (Shift 10 cm), Del delete, Ctrl+D duplicate, Esc deselect.
    • Views: 1 3D, 2 Plan, 3 Walk. The Cut slider slices off the top of the scan so you can see into rooms.
    • Measure: M, then click two points. Esc to stop; click a measurement label to delete it.
    • Tidy scan: Erase / Restore paint over the scan; Box removes or restores everything inside a box.
    • Everything saves in this browser. Use Layouts → Share to send a design to someone else.
